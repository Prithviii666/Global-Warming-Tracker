The Devastating Consequences of Global Warming

🌡️ Rising Temperatures

Global average temperatures have risen by 1.1°C since pre-industrial times. Heat waves are becoming more frequent and intense, causing droughts, wildfires, and making many regions uninhabitable.

🌊 Sea Level Rise

Melting ice caps and thermal expansion cause sea levels to rise 3.3mm annually. Coastal cities face flooding, and entire island nations risk disappearing underwater within decades.

🌪️ Extreme Weather

Climate change intensifies hurricanes, tornadoes, and storms. Weather patterns become unpredictable, causing massive property damage and threatening lives worldwide.

🐧 Ecosystem Collapse

Arctic ice melting destroys polar bear habitats. Ocean acidification kills coral reefs. Species extinction rates are 100-1,000 times higher than natural rates.

🌾 Food Security Crisis

Changing weather patterns disrupt agriculture. Crop yields decrease due to droughts, floods, and shifting seasons. Food prices soar, threatening global food security.

💧 Water Scarcity

Glaciers that supply freshwater to billions are melting. Droughts intensify while some regions face devastating floods. Clean water access becomes increasingly scarce.
